Tando Beach Resort

A serene beachfront resort in Barangay Libjo, Infanta, Quezon. Perfect for family outings, beach activities, and scenic seaside relaxation with views of the Pacific Ocean.

Overview

Tando Beach Resort offers a long sandy shore, peaceful surroundings, and a relaxed vibe ideal for swimming, picnics, photography, and family gatherings.

Location & Accessibility

Located in Purok Dahlia, Barangay Libjo, Infanta, Quezon. Accessible via Marikina–Infanta Highway or Famy–Infanta Road. Tricycles and jeepneys can bring visitors from the town proper.

Geography & Natural Features

The resort features coastal scenery, ocean breeze, tropical vegetation, and calm sea conditions depending on the season.

Facilities & Activities

  • Swimming and beach wading
  • Picnics and family gatherings
  • Photography and sightseeing
  • Beach cottages and shaded areas
  • Restrooms and changing rooms

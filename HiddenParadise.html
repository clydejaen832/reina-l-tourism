Hidden Paradise Resort

A nature-focused getaway in Barangay Magsaysay, Infanta, Quezon with fresh-water pools, mountain scenery, and affordable accommodations perfect for day tours, overnight stays, and family outings.

Overview

Hidden Paradise Resort is a budget-friendly nature destination with fresh-water pools, cottages, and camping options. It offers a relaxing environment surrounded by the Sierra Madre Mountains.

Location & Accessibility

Located at Km 12, Barangay Magsaysay, Infanta. Accessible via Marikina–Infanta Highway (Marilaque Road). Public transport, private vehicles, or motorcycles can reach the site with minimal hiking.

Geography & Natural Features

The resort features clean fresh-water pools, tropical vegetation, and open-air areas for relaxation, with serene mountain views and a quiet atmosphere due to minimal phone signal coverage.

Facilities & Activities

  • Cottages, rooms, and camping sites
  • Swimming in natural pools
  • Banana boat rides and volleyball
  • Bonfires and karaoke sessions
  • Picnic and photography spots

Tips & Best Time to Visit

  • Best during dry season (November–April)
  • Day tours or overnight stays recommended
  • Watch for uneven terrain near pools and nature trails
  • Keep the environment clean and quiet

Cultural & Local Significance

Supports local tourism and promotes eco-friendly recreation in Infanta. Managed privately through Facebook and email for bookings.

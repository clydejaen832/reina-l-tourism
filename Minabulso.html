Minabulso Falls

Minabulso Falls is a stunning multi-tiered waterfall located in Barangay Magsaysay, General Nakar, Quezon. Known for its crystal-clear cascading waters and natural rock pools, it offers a serene escape into the lush Sierra Madre forest, attracting hikers, nature lovers, and adventure seekers alike.

Overview

Minabulso Falls features several cascading tiers with varying heights, flowing into natural pools suitable for swimming. The surrounding forest creates a cool, shaded environment and provides excellent opportunities for birdwatching, photography, and enjoying the rich biodiversity of General Nakar. The falls’ name, “Minabulso,” is derived from the forceful burst of water that characterizes its lower tiers.

Location & Accessibility

Located in the upland barangay of Magsaysay, General Nakar, visitors can reach the jump-off point via land transport from the town proper. From there, a guided trek of 1–2 hours follows forest trails and river crossings. Hikers are advised to wear proper footwear and carry sufficient water, as the trail includes rugged terrain and occasional slippery areas.

Geography & Natural Features

The falls flow over naturally sculpted rock formations with multiple plunge pools, creating an ideal environment for swimming and relaxation. Dense tropical forests, streams, and rivers surround the site. Wildlife sightings, including native birds, butterflies, and small forest mammals, are common. The combination of shaded areas, fresh water, and rocky formations provides both scenic beauty and adventure challenges for visitors.

Tourism & Visitor Information

Visitors can enjoy hiking, swimming, river crossings, and photography. Picnicking is possible at designated rest areas near the trailhead. Local guides are available for safety, and environmental fees may apply. Visitors must follow Leave No Trace principles, respect wildlife, and avoid damaging the vegetation. The falls support eco-tourism in the area, providing income for local guides and promoting conservation of the natural environment.

  • Designated jump-off and registration area
  • Guided trekking recommended for safety
  • Basic rest areas near the trailhead
  • Minimal development at the falls to preserve nature

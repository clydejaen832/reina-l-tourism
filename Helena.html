Helena Beach Resort

A beachfront resort in Dinahican, Infanta, Quezon, offering peaceful seaside views, family-friendly amenities, and accommodations ideal for day trips or overnight stays.

Overview

Helena Beach Resort is located along Dinahican Beach, featuring fine sand, calm coastal waters, and a peaceful environment. Perfect for families, groups, and photography enthusiasts.

Location & Accessibility

Purok Buyan, Barangay Dinahican, Infanta. Accessible via private vehicles or public transport to Infanta, then via local roads to Dinahican. No hiking needed.

Geography & Natural Features

Along the shoreline of Dinahican Beach, featuring calm coastal waters, tropical climate, open ocean views, and scenic sunrise/moonrise experiences.

Facilities & Activities

  • Beachfront cottages and rooms (fan / AC)
  • Swimming, relaxation, and beach photography
  • Bonfire area and videoke for rent
  • Playground for kids, grilling area, kitchen use
  • Free Wi-Fi and parking for guests

Balagbag Falls

Balagbag Falls is one of the most visited natural attractions in Real, Quezon, known for its refreshing mountain waters, scenic surroundings, and easy accessibility along the Real–Infanta Road.

Overview

Balagbag Falls is a naturally formed two-tiered waterfall flowing from the Sierra Madre Mountains. Its lower tier features a clear natural pool ideal for swimming and cliff jumping, while the upper tier offers a calm space for relaxation and scenic views.

Location & Accessibility

Located in Barangay Malapad, Real, Quezon, Balagbag Falls is easily reached via private or public transport. A short five-minute walk from the drop-off point leads directly to the waterfall.

Geography & Natural Features

The waterfall has two tiers, rocky terrain, lush vegetation, and cold clear water year-round. The upper tier offers panoramic views of the surrounding forested mountains.

Tourism & Visitor Information

Visitors enjoy swimming, hiking, sightseeing, and photography. Basic amenities are available, and a minimal entrance fee helps support maintenance and safety.

  • Best time to visit: November–May
  • Be cautious on slippery rocks
  • Practice environmental responsibility

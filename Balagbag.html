Balagbag Falls

Balagbag Falls is one of the most visited natural attractions in Real, Quezon, known for its refreshing mountain waters, scenic surroundings, and easy accessibility along the Real–Infanta Road.

Overview

Balagbag Falls is a naturally formed two-tiered waterfall flowing from the Sierra Madre Mountains. Its lower tier features a clear natural pool that is ideal for swimming and cliff jumping, while the upper tier offers a calmer space where visitors can relax and enjoy the surrounding forested landscape. Due to its proximity to the main highway, the falls attract both tourists and locals seeking a quick nature escape.

Location & Accessibility

The falls are located in Barangay Malapad, Real, Quezon, near the Real–Infanta Road and within the foothills of the Sierra Madre Mountain range. Visitors can reach the area using private vehicles or public transportation. From the drop-off point, a short five-minute walk leads directly to the lower tier of the waterfall, making it one of the most accessible waterfalls in the municipality.

Geography & Natural Features

Balagbag Falls consists of two cascading levels surrounded by rocky terrain and lush tropical vegetation. The lower tier forms a natural pool approximately five meters deep, while the upper tier provides elevated views of the surrounding mountains. The water remains cold and clear throughout the year, especially during the dry season.

Tourism & Visitor Information

Balagbag Falls offers various recreational activities for visitors of all ages. The area is commonly used for swimming, sightseeing, hiking, photography, and relaxation. Basic facilities such as shaded resting areas and picnic spots are available near the site. A minimal entrance fee may be collected to help maintain cleanliness and safety.

  • Best time to visit: November to May (dry season)
  • Observe safety precautions on slippery rocks
  • Practice proper waste disposal and environmental care

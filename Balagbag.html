Balagbag Falls

A two-tiered waterfall in Real, Quezon known for its clear mountain waters, natural pools, and scenic views.

Overview

Balagbag Falls is a popular tourist destination in Real, Quezon, featuring cold and clear water flowing from the Sierra Madre Mountains. The lower tier has a natural pool ideal for swimming and cliff jumping, while the upper tier provides a peaceful area for sightseeing and relaxation.

Location & Accessibility

📍 Location

Barangay Malapad, Real, Quezon, along the Real–Infanta Road near the Sierra Madre Mountains.

🚗 Accessibility

Accessible via private vehicles or public transportation. A 5-minute walk leads to the falls.

🌿 Natural Features

Two-tiered waterfall, rocky terrain, natural pool, clear mountain water, and lush vegetation.

Tourism Information

🎒 Activities

  • Swimming
  • Cliff jumping
  • Hiking
  • Photography

💰 Fees & Rules

Minimal entrance fee may apply. Visitors are encouraged to follow safety and cleanliness rules.

☀️ Best Time to Visit

Dry season from November to May for safer access and better swimming conditions.

Cultural & Environmental Significance

Balagbag Falls is part of the natural heritage of Real, Quezon. It serves as a recreational area for locals and promotes eco-tourism and awareness of the biodiversity of the Sierra Madre Mountains.

Balagbag Falls

Overview: Balagbag Falls is a popular two-tiered waterfall in Real, Quezon, known for its cold, clear water flowing from the Sierra Madre Mountains. It features a lower natural pool ideal for swimming and cliff jumping, as well as a scenic upper tier for relaxing and enjoying mountain views.

Location: Barangay Malapad, Real, Quezon, near the Real–Infanta Road and the Sierra Madre Mountain range.

Accessibility: Accessible via private vehicle or public transport. A short five-minute walk from the drop-off point leads to the falls.

Geography & Natural Features: Two-tiered waterfall with a 5-meter-deep natural pool, rocky terrain, clear mountain water, and lush tropical vegetation.

History / Background: Naturally formed from Sierra Madre streams. The name “Balagbag” comes from a local term describing splashing water.

Tourism & Activities: Swimming, cliff jumping, hiking, sightseeing, photography, and nature relaxation.

Facilities & Amenities: Picnic areas, shaded spots, parking near the road, and limited local food stalls.

Fees & Regulations: Minimal entrance fee. Visitors must follow safety rules and environmental guidelines.

Management: Managed by Barangay Malapad LGU with local community support.

Best Time to Visit: Dry season (November–May).

Safety & Environmental Protection: Beware of slippery rocks, avoid swimming during heavy rain, and practice proper waste disposal.

Cultural Significance: A local recreational site that promotes eco-tourism and awareness of Sierra Madre biodiversity.

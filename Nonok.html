Nonok Falls (Lunok Falls)

Nonok Falls, also known as Lunok Falls, is a breathtaking ~10-meter-high waterfall located in Brgy. Tignoan, Real, Quezon. Known for its wide and deep catch basin, it is a popular destination for swimming and cliff jumping.

Overview

This easily accessible waterfall is surrounded by lush forest and tall trees, creating a refreshing shaded environment. Just a short 5–15 minute walk from the Real–Mauban Road leads visitors to its strong flowing waters and wide basin, making it ideal for adventure seekers and nature lovers.

Location & Accessibility

Located in Barangay Tignoan, Real, Quezon, the falls can be reached by a less-than-10-minute walk from the main Real–Mauban Road. The short trek makes it convenient for visitors looking for a quick nature getaway.

Geography & Natural Features

The waterfall is surrounded by remarkable rock formations and dense forest. The cold, fresh water flows into a deep natural swimming area that appears inviting yet requires caution. The towering trees provide natural shade, enhancing the peaceful and cool atmosphere.

Tourism & Visitor Information

Visitors can relax in open nipa huts, enjoy picnics, swim, and explore the unique rock formations. Cliff diving is popular but may be risky due to the area being less developed and minimally maintained.

  • Entrance fee: Approx. PHP 20–40 per person
  • Parking fee: Around PHP 20
  • Managed under the LGU of Real (Aksyon Diretso agenda)
  • Exercise caution on slippery rocks
  • Practice proper waste disposal

Pinlac Bridge & Pinlac Falls

Pinlac Bridge is a landmark crossing over the Agus River along Marilaque Highway, while nearby Pinlac Falls offers cascading freshwater pools and lush forest surroundings. Ideal for eco-tourism, swimming, trekking, and photography.

Overview

Pinlac Bridge is a key transport link connecting Infanta to Rizal and Metro Manila. Pinlac Falls is a scenic waterfall nearby with multiple cascading layers and natural pools, perfect for swimming, trekking, picnics, and photography. Both spots highlight the natural beauty of the Sierra Madre mountains and forested riverbanks.

Location & Accessibility

Located in Barangay Magsaysay, Infanta, Quezon, Pinlac Bridge is accessible via Marilaque Highway by motorcycle, tricycle, jeepney, or private car. Pinlac Falls requires a short walk from Barangay Pinagrealan along a marked trail, suitable for casual hikers and nature enthusiasts.

Geography & Natural Features

The bridge spans the Agus River, originating from the Sierra Madre mountains. Pinlac Falls features fresh, clean waters cascading into natural pools, surrounded by lush tropical vegetation, rolling hills, and forest trails. The serene environment provides ideal conditions for eco-tourism and photography.

Facilities & Activities

  • Swimming and wading in natural pools
  • Trekking along forest trails
  • Picnicking near riverbanks
  • Basic cottages for resting
  • Photography and sightseeing

Bagumbong Falls

Refreshing freshwater waterfall in Real, Quezon, ideal for swimming, trekking, and picnicking amidst lush forested hills.

Overview

Bagumbong Falls is a scenic natural destination, offering cool pools, cascading waterfalls, and a peaceful environment perfect for nature escapes.

Location & Accessibility

Located in Barangay Tanauan, Real, Quezon. Accessible via a short trek from the jump-off point near the main road. Footwear suitable for wet rocks is recommended.

Geography & Natural Features

Surrounded by forested hills, the waterfall has natural pools and lush vegetation, offering a tranquil and refreshing setting for visitors.

Tourism & Activities

  • Swimming in natural pools
  • Trekking and nature watching
  • Picnicking in shaded areas
  • Respect local eco-rules

Eco Adventure Park

A nature-focused eco-tourism site in Barangay Magsaysay, Infanta offering river swims, forest trekking, bamboo rafting, and wildlife experiences in lush surroundings.

Overview

Eco Adventure Park features natural water pools, open green areas, and forest trails. Perfect for family outings, adventure, and nature exploration in Infanta.

Location & Accessibility

Located at Km 2, Barangay Magsaysay, Infanta. Accessible via private vehicle or public commute along local roads leading from the highway.

Geography & Natural Features

Rural area near the Sierra Madre foothills, tropical climate, lush vegetation, natural water pools, and diverse flora and fauna including fruit bats at the nearby mangrove park.

Facilities & Activities

  • Swimming and kayaking in natural pools
  • Picnic areas and open green spaces
  • Bamboo rafting and forest trekking
  • Cottages and transient rooms for overnight stays

Tips & Best Time to Visit

  • Dry season (November–May) preferred
  • Follow safety guidelines for water activities
  • Observe cleanliness and environmental rules

Cultural & Local Significance

Supports local eco-tourism, highlights Infanta’s natural beauty, and promotes family-friendly outdoor recreation while contributing to the local economy.

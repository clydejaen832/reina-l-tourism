About This Website

TouReina is a tourism website created to showcase selected destinations in Real, Quezon. This platform serves as our final project output in the subject Empowerment Technology, where we applied our knowledge in web design, layout structuring, and digital content creation.

Project Background

This website was developed as part of our requirements in Empowerment Technology. The goal was to create a functional and informative website that demonstrates our understanding of HTML, CSS, and basic interactive elements. Through this project, we learned how technology can be used to organize information clearly and present it in a visually appealing way.

Why We Created This

We chose to focus on tourism in Real, Quezon to highlight the natural beauty of local destinations. Our aim was to build a clean, user-friendly website that provides helpful information for visitors while also demonstrating our technical skills.

Challenges We Faced

One of the main struggles our group experienced was early coordination. At the beginning, it was difficult to align our schedules, divide tasks properly, and finalize our design ideas. There were moments of misunderstanding and delays, especially while combining different suggestions into one final concept.

How We Worked Together

Despite the challenges, we managed to improve our communication and teamwork. We distributed responsibilities based on each member’s strengths and made sure to update one another regularly. By cooperating, supporting each other, and staying focused on our goal, we were able to complete this project successfully.

  • Improved communication and coordination
  • Clear task distribution
  • Collaborative problem-solving
  • Shared responsibility until completion
